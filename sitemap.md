# Website Sitemap and Architecture

## Public routes

* `/` Home. Dual audience entry point for learners and businesses.
* `/training` Training catalogue. Search and topic filters.
* `/training/[slug]` Programme detail template. Six current or historical public offers are represented with approval states.
* `/services` Professional services overview.
* `/services/[slug]` Service detail template for five evidenced service areas.
* `/about` Verified company story and open approvals.
* `/resources` Practical guide landing page.
* `/faq` Learner and business questions with FAQ structured data.
* `/contact` Verified contact channels and business enquiry form.
* `/register` Training interest form with programme selection.
* `/legal/privacy` Privacy draft.
* `/legal/terms` Terms draft.
* `/legal/academic-integrity` Research writing boundary.

## Supporting route documents

* `/manus-routes.json` Platform route manifest.
* `/sitemap.xml` Generated route sitemap.
* `/robots.txt` Generated crawl directive.

## Future routes

Projects and case studies, business capability assessment, programme comparison, and approved location pages should be added only when the business has genuine evidence and a clear visitor need.

## Internal linking

Home links to Training, Services, Resources, FAQ, Registration, and Contact. Catalogue cards link to programme details and registration. Service cards link to detail pages and enquiry. Resources link to related decisions. Footer links keep legal pages and next actions available on every route.
