# Phase 2 Programme Research

Research was completed against the public Grand Galaxy site and its published registration or product pages. The public implementation uses only supported details. Missing dates, modules, instructors, certificates, entry requirements, and prices remain omitted rather than guessed.

## Programme findings

### Digital Marketing
Grand Galaxy presents this as a live session offer with a registration form. The form says a commitment fee is required for access to the main platform, but does not publish the amount, curriculum, duration, level, instructor, certificate policy, or entry requirements. Sources: https://grandgalaxy.ng/ and https://forms.gle/dxdYkapQ1pyGVoF88.

## Reference experience

Coursera was reviewed for information hierarchy rather than copied content. Its public homepage uses direct audience paths, course discovery, clear subject framing, structured outcomes, practical context, frequently asked questions, and learner focused actions. The rebuilt Grand Galaxy pages use the same general principles with original structure, copy, brand identity, and imagery. Source: https://www.coursera.org/.

## Public content boundary

Historical dates from older pages are not presented as current cohort dates. Individual product prices are used only where the current catalogue or product page supplied a price. No unsupported instructor names, employment outcomes, accreditation, or certificate promises were added.
