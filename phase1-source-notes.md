# Phase 1 Source Notes

The official Grand Galaxy homepage was reviewed at https://grandgalaxy.ng/. It presents La Grande Galaxie Consult, links to About, Blog, Contact, Courses, Our Services, Shop, and a WhatsApp chat. The homepage visibly features Digital Marketing, Web Development, Data Analysis, Writing, Public Speaking, and UI UX categories.

The official courses page was reviewed at https://grandgalaxy.ng/courses/. It describes courses as practical and industry relevant across multiple fields and directs visitors to WhatsApp for more course information.

The official shop catalogue was reviewed at https://grandgalaxy.ng/courses-and-services/. It showed six products at the time of review: Thirty Day Excel Challenge at ₦16,500, Data Analysis with SPSS and Excel at ₦35,000, Data Analysis with SQL, Power BI and Tableau at ₦20,000 sale price, Data Science and Analytics Mentorship Program at ₦100,000 sale price, Exclusive Group for SPSS Simplified at ₦16,500, and R and Stata Data Analysis Training at ₦25,500. The implementation uses these details as visible catalogue information and links each product to its official source page.

The official services page was reviewed at https://grandgalaxy.ng/our-services/. It visibly describes Web Development Services, Online Tutoring Services, Mobile App Development Services, Data Analytics Services, and Research Writing Services. The service pages use the published scope as the basis for customer facing summaries.

Coursera was reviewed at https://www.coursera.org/. Its current public experience uses clear audience routes, a catalogue search prompt, category groupings, product cards with title and type, and multiple discovery sections. The rebuild uses these general discovery principles without copying Coursera branding or proprietary design.

Visual assets were sourced from approved official Grand Galaxy media URLs where available and from downloaded Unsplash search results labelled as free photos for technology workspaces, business collaboration, public speaking, research writing, analytics dashboards, travel training, project management, and interface design. The assets are stored locally in `public/assets` and converted to WebP for performance.
