# Brand Design System

## Source of truth

The supplied La Grande Galaxie Logo Guideline by Mezue Studios, dated 3 March 2024, is the visual source of truth. The supplied vertical black logo is used as an image asset without redraw, distortion, recoloring, or proportion changes.

## Colors

* Primary deep blue: `#140D91`
* Accent bright blue: `#0269E0`
* Supporting orange: `#E3832E`
* Supporting green: `#00CE38`
* Dark ink: `#0F1219`
* White and pale neutral surfaces for breathing room

Deep blue anchors identity and trust. Bright blue marks actions and links. Orange is used for attention cues. Green is reserved for positive or approved status. Dark ink supports text and footer contrast.

## Typography

Prompt is the primary typeface. Bold supports major headings. Semibold supports section headings. Medium supports navigation and buttons. Regular supports body copy. System sans serif fallback is used when Prompt is not available.

## Components

The site uses a sticky header, white logo plate, responsive navigation, pill buttons, status chips, breadcrumb navigation, pathway cards, programme cards, service cards, resource cards, approval boxes, accessible accordions, validated forms, and deep blue conversion bands.

## Public writing rules

Visible copy avoids hyphen, en dash, and em dash characters. Claims are direct and conversational. The experience labels verified information, historical reference, and details needing approval.
