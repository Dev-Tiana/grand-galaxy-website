# Verified Company Information Report

## Verified public facts

The live website is a reachable WordPress and WooCommerce property presenting La Grande Galaxie Consult or La Grande Galaxie Consults. Public profile evidence strongly indicates that the LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, and grandgalaxy.ng presence represent the same operating brand, although the legal entity relationship is not established by the reviewed sources.

The visible service areas are web development, mobile app development, online tutoring and training, data analytics or professional data analysis, and research writing. The public website also exposes learning and product pages for Excel, SPSS, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Python, R, Stata, mentorship, travel industry training, and professional development.

The public site publishes `info@grandgalaxy.ng` and `+234 810 504 3903` on course or product content. It provides a contact form. A linked Google Form requests name, email, location, phone, an importance rating, and payment readiness, and states that sign in is required.

The inspected public pages do not publish a street address, city, state, office location, opening hours, or map. Explicit delivery references include Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp, and Zoom.

## Not verified

The following are intentionally not presented as established facts on the new website: legal name, registration number, owners, founders, directors, founding year, registered address, operating city, complete team roster, current cohort dates, current fees, current payment account, refunds, certificates, accreditations, testimonials, job outcomes, salary outcomes, partner credentials, or current availability.

## Recommended client approvals

Confirm the canonical public name, legal identity, operating location, service area, team and trainer profiles, current programme catalogue, fees, delivery channels, certificate policy, payment method, refund and cancellation terms, privacy contact, form processing, data retention, testimonials, credentials, and proof examples. Confirm whether the current business wants a CMS such as Sanity or prefers file based updates.
