# Phase 1 Implementation Summary

The second instruction pass has been implemented on the managed website project.

## Main changes

* Reworked the homepage around the requested direct headline, learner and business pathways, training and service sections, brand story, resource cards, FAQ, and dual conversion band.
* Replaced placeholder and internal approval language across the public routes with direct visitor facing copy.
* Expanded the catalogue to the 15 named offers plus three additional official shop products that were verified on the official shop page.
* Added reusable individual programme pages with category, type, format, listed price when sourced, details, image, official source link, and registration action.
* Added relevant image assets for every programme and service card where an appropriate visual was available. Official Grand Galaxy media was used for the hero, service imagery, and shop products. Selected reusable Unsplash results were downloaded and converted to local WebP assets for other topics.
* Added five complete resource guide pages with useful internal calls to action.
* Added a focused business enquiry form with validation, honeypot protection, approved email handoff, and clear confirmation state.
* Added mobile responsive card layouts, photographic hero treatment, subtle entrance motion, hover states, reduced motion support, and improved visual hierarchy.
