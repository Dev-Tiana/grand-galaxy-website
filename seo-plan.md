# SEO and Search Intent Plan

## Approach

This plan uses qualitative intent groups only. It does not claim search volume, rankings, or keyword difficulty. Validate the seed list later with Google Keyword Planner and Search Console after the business confirms its current services and operating locations.

## Page mapping

* Home: La Grande Galaxie, La Grande Galaxie Nigeria, professional training and technology services Nigeria, skills and digital solutions.
* Training: professional training Nigeria, digital skills training Nigeria, corporate training Nigeria, online professional training Nigeria.
* Programme details: audience and skill specific queries such as data analytics training Nigeria, Excel training, SPSS training, SQL and Power BI training, only when the programme is current and approved.
* Services: technology consulting Nigeria, web development services Nigeria, mobile app development Nigeria, data analytics services Nigeria, online tutoring and training.
* Service details: customer problem queries mapped to each verified service, with deliverables, process, boundaries, evidence status, and enquiry action.
* Business: custom staff training, capability assessment, custom cohort, technology support for organizations.
* Resources: digital skills checklists, training planning guidance, presentation skills, business writing, and choosing a technology partner, only where aligned with real expertise.
* About and Contact: brand identity, verified contact, service area only after confirmation, author and team evidence after approval.
* FAQ: course format, prerequisites, certificates, fees, customization, support, refund, privacy, and post project support.

## Technical implementation notes

Every indexable route has meaningful body HTML, a unique title, a unique meta description, an H1, internal links, and a breadcrumb or clear page context. Canonical tags and absolute social URLs are omitted until a real public origin is configured. The route manifest, sitemap and robots documents describe the intended public paths. Structured data is limited to visible Organization, Service, Course, Article, FAQ and BreadcrumbList information that is accurate at the time of publication.

Use one canonical business name policy. Archive or redirect stale 2024 and 2025 programme pages rather than allowing outdated cohort pages to compete with current pages. Do not create city pages until the business confirms the locations it actually serves.

## AI search readiness

Use direct headings, concise answers, consistent terminology, named authors and trainers after approval, visible update dates, clear service boundaries, and evidence links. Make the difference between verified information and pending approval obvious to visitors. Avoid hidden text, keyword stuffing, fabricated expertise, unsupported reviews, and job or salary guarantees.

## Measurement plan

Prepare events for catalogue search, filter use, programme detail views, registration starts, registration submits, service enquiry submits, phone clicks, WhatsApp clicks, and resource engagement. Treat analytics as prepared until a real analytics property is connected and tested. Review qualified enquiries and completed registrations rather than traffic alone.

## Ninety day plan

### First thirty days

Confirm identity, location, services, current programme list, dates, fees, delivery, payment, privacy, refund, credentials, testimonials, and team evidence. Connect Search Console and analytics after consent and ownership are handled. Replace pending status labels with approved facts.

### Days thirty one to sixty

Publish the most valuable programme and service detail pages. Add approved trainer and project proof. Refresh internal links from useful guides to conversion pages. Validate filters, forms, phone links, WhatsApp links, and structured data.

### Days sixty one to ninety

Use Search Console queries and qualified enquiry data to improve titles, descriptions, headings, and resource topics. Consolidate pages that overlap. Add only the location pages and content clusters the business can support with genuine service coverage and evidence.
