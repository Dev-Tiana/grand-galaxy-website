# Research Summary

## Verified findings

The live site is a reachable WordPress and WooCommerce property presenting La Grande Galaxie Consult or La Grande Galaxie Consults. The strongest public identity match connects the site with the LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook profiles through matching names, services, links, and payment account wording. This supports an operating brand match, not a proven legal entity match.

The visible service areas are web development, mobile app development, online tutoring and training, data analytics or professional data analysis, and research writing. Public pages also describe training and products related to Excel, SPSS, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Python, R, Stata, mentorship, travel industry training, and professional development.

The site publishes `info@grandgalaxy.ng` and `+234 810 504 3903` on course or product content. The Contact page has a name, email, and message form. A linked Google Form requests name, email, location, phone, an importance rating, and payment readiness, and states that sign in is required.

The inspected site pages do not publish a street address, city, state, office location, hours, or map. Explicit delivery references include online channels such as Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp, and Zoom.

The live shop exposes six products. The current shop observations list #30DAYSEXCELCHALLENGE at ₦16,500, SPSS and Excel at ₦35,000, SQL, Power BI and Tableau at a ₦20,000 sale price from ₦45,000, the mentorship programme at a ₦100,000 sale price from ₦120,000, SPSS Simplified at ₦16,500, and R and Stata training at ₦25,500. Older course pages contain historical dates and conflicting prices, so the rebuild does not publish those details as current.

## Unconfirmed information that requires client approval

Legal name, registration record, ownership, founding date, registered address, operating location, founders, directors, complete team roster, current service boundaries, current programme list, cohort dates, fees, delivery channels, certificate policy, payment details, refund terms, data handling, testimonials, credentials, accreditation, career support, job outcomes, and partner claims remain unconfirmed.

Claims attributed to La Plage Meta Verse about CPD status, ISO 9001, Association of Healthcare Trainers UK membership, or ACTD accreditation must not be presented as La Grande Galaxie credentials without documentary proof.

## Design and product conclusions

Coursera provides useful patterns for progressive discovery, clear catalogue filters, comparison metadata, persistent primary actions, predictable detail page hierarchy, and distinct audience routes. The rebuild adapts those patterns with an original constellation system and the supplied brand identity.

Competitor research suggests that decision ready training cards should expose audience, duration, delivery, current cohort status, fee or payment plan when approved, practical work, credentials, and next action. A separate B2B funnel should support capability assessment, custom cohorts, proposal requests, and consultations.

The first release therefore uses a dual audience story, a searchable catalogue with approval status labels, reusable service pages, safe enquiry and registration forms, and prominent evidence governance.
